# Reinforced Driver Airbag Cushion with Dual-Seam Tear Prevention

## Abstract
An improved driver's frontal airbag cushion incorporates a reinforced seam structure and a stress-distribution panel to prevent tearing during deployment in 2021-2025 Model S and Model X vehicles. The design uses 420-denier nylon fabric with silicone coating, dual parallel seams spaced 8 mm apart, and an internal tether panel that limits peak fabric stress to under 35 MPa. This ensures compliance with FMVSS 208 and 212 without requiring full assembly replacement.

## Problem
The driver's airbag cushion tears at the seam during rapid inflation from the steering wheel module. Tearing occurs due to concentrated hoop stress at the initial fold line exceeding fabric tensile strength of 250 N/cm. Resulting non-compliance with FMVSS 208 and 212 leaves the occupant unprotected in frontal impacts.

## Summary of the invention
The airbag cushion (10) comprises a main panel (12) of coated nylon fabric folded and joined by a primary seam (14) and a parallel secondary seam (16) spaced 8 mm apart. An internal stress-distribution panel (18) of 210-denier fabric is sewn across the deployment fold zone with 6 mm stitch length. A vent (20) of 40 mm diameter is located 120 mm from the inflator neck (22). The assembly mounts to steering wheel armature (24) with four M6 bolts torqued to 10 Nm.

## Claims
1. A driver's frontal airbag cushion comprising a main fabric panel folded along a deployment axis and joined by a primary seam and a secondary seam spaced 8 mm apart, further comprising an internal stress-distribution panel sewn across the fold zone with 6 mm stitch length, wherein the fabric is 420-denier nylon with silicone coating having tensile strength at least 300 N/cm.

2. The airbag cushion of claim 1 wherein the stress-distribution panel is 210-denier nylon and covers a zone extending 60 mm on each side of the fold line.

3. The airbag cushion of claim 1 further comprising a 40 mm diameter vent positioned 120 mm from the inflator neck.

4. The airbag cushion of claim 1 wherein the primary and secondary seams are lock-stitch type with 4 stitches per cm using 150-denier thread.

5. The airbag cushion of claim 1 mounted to a steering wheel armature by four M6 bolts at 10 Nm torque, with the cushion folded to a pack height of 45 mm.

6. The airbag cushion of claim 1 wherein the coating thickness is 0.15 mm to achieve gas permeability below 5 L/m²/min at 20 kPa.

## Detailed description
The main panel (12) is cut from 420-denier nylon fabric with 0.15 mm silicone coating on the inner surface. The panel is folded along the deployment axis and secured by primary seam (14) using lock stitch at 4 stitches per cm. Secondary seam (16) runs parallel 8 mm outward. Stress-distribution panel (18) of 210-denier fabric is sewn inside the fold zone extending 60 mm on each side of the fold line with 6 mm stitch length. This reduces peak fabric stress from 45 MPa to under 35 MPa during 35 ms inflation. Vent (20) of 40 mm diameter is located 120 mm from inflator neck (22) to control internal pressure to 120 kPa peak. The folded pack is secured with a tear seam cover (26) that opens at 80 N. The assembly attaches to steering wheel armature (24) via four M6 bolts torqued to 10 Nm. Failure mode of seam rupture is mitigated by the secondary seam maintaining integrity if the primary seam begins to fail. Gas leakage through coating is limited to 5 L/m²/min at 20 kPa. The design maintains deployment time of 30-35 ms to full volume of 60 liters.
