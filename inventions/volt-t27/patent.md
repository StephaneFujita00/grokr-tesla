# Wiper Arm Elbow Connector With Integrated Flow Channel And Filter Screen

## Abstract
An elbow connector for a windshield washer system in a vehicle wiper arm has a molded body with an internal straight flow channel of 3.2 mm diameter, a 40-mesh stainless steel screen at the inlet, and barbed fittings with 0.5 mm wall thickness. The design prevents molding flash or debris from obstructing nozzle orifices, ensuring continuous fluid delivery at 1.8 bar pressure. The connector attaches between the supply hose and wiper arm spray head.

## Problem
In Model Y vehicles, the hose connector at the wiper arm elbow can contain internal molding flash or misalignment that blocks the fluid path to the windshield washer nozzles. This reduces or stops fluid flow, violating FMVSS 104 and impairing visibility.

## Prior art
- KR20060067225A, Washer liquid injector of automobile, describes a body-mounted injector without addressing connector blockage in wiper arm elbows.
- KR101063238B1, Vehicle washer hose coupling, covers hose connections near the engine but does not include internal filters or straight channels to prevent flash obstruction at the nozzle.

## Summary of the invention
The invention provides an elbow connector (10) with a continuous internal flow channel (12) of constant 3.2 mm diameter, an inlet screen (14) of 40-mesh 304 stainless steel, and outlet barbs sized for 4 mm ID hose. The channel is formed by precision core pins during injection molding of glass-filled nylon 6/6, eliminating flash at the 90-degree bend. A pressure sensor port (16) allows optional monitoring.

## Claims
1. An elbow connector (10) for a vehicle windshield washer system comprising a molded body having an inlet barb (18), an outlet barb (20), and an internal flow channel (12) of constant 3.2 mm diameter extending from inlet to outlet, wherein a 40-mesh stainless steel screen (14) is insert-molded at the inlet.
2. The elbow connector of claim 1, wherein the molded body is glass-filled nylon 6/6 with 0.5 mm minimum wall thickness around the flow channel.
3. The elbow connector of claim 1, wherein the outlet barb (20) has a 4.0 mm outer diameter and 1.2 mm barb height for retention in a 4 mm ID hose.
4. The elbow connector of claim 1, further comprising a pressure sensor port (16) intersecting the flow channel (12) at 45 degrees.
5. The elbow connector of claim 1, wherein the screen (14) is retained by ultrasonic welding of a cap (22) after insert placement.
6. A method of assembling a wiper arm washer system comprising pressing the inlet barb (18) of the elbow connector (10) into a supply hose and the outlet barb (20) into a wiper arm nozzle manifold, the flow channel (12) maintaining at least 95 percent open area after 500 thermal cycles from -40 C to 85 C.

## Detailed description
The elbow connector (10) is injection molded from 30 percent glass-filled nylon 6/6 at 280 C melt temperature with core pins of 3.2 mm diameter held to +/- 0.05 mm tolerance to form the straight flow channel (12). Inlet barb (18) accepts 5 mm ID supply hose with 0.8 mm interference fit. Outlet barb (20) inserts 8 mm into the wiper arm manifold. The 40-mesh screen (14) of 0.25 mm wire diameter 304 stainless steel is placed in a counterbore at the inlet and secured by ultrasonic welding of cap (22) at 20 kHz for 0.8 seconds. The pressure sensor port (16) accepts a 1/8 NPT fitting for optional 0-5 bar transducer. In operation, washer fluid at 1.8 bar flows through the screen (14) which captures particles larger than 0.4 mm, then through channel (12) to the nozzles. Failure mode of flash blockage is eliminated by the constant-diameter core pin design. Thermal cycling tests confirm no deformation exceeding 0.1 mm after 500 cycles. All dimensions stated in claims are maintained in production with CpK greater than 1.67.
